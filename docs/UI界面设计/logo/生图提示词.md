# PixoFold（轻图）Logo 生图记录

日期：2026-09-20。

- 执行方式：内置 `image_gen` 生图模型。
- 初始任务类型：`logo-brand`，基于参考图生成新设计。
- 参考来源：png-palettes 的 `src/assets/icons/svg/logo.svg`，副本见 [参考 SVG](参考-png-palettes-logo.svg)。
- 模型输入：[参考 PNG](参考-png-palettes-logo.png)。该文件由原 SVG 等比例渲染得到，仅用于向模型提供参考。
- 输出为 PNG 设计稿；提示词中的矢量风格指视觉效果，不表示输出文件是 SVG。

## 首轮完整提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a single new application logo / app icon for PixoFold（轻图）, a modern local desktop image compressor.
Input image 1: visual reference ONLY, the existing png-palettes logo. Inherit its clear blue-and-white identity and idea of making images compact, but create a distinct, simpler new symbol rather than tracing the old logo.
Primary concept: a folded photograph. Design one bold white geometric picture symbol with an elegant diagonal folded corner, so the fold itself suggests an image being neatly compacted. The fold is the signature feature, referring to "Fold" in PixoFold. Integrate a very simple mountain-shaped negative space into the picture, using large clear shapes rather than fine detail. Keep the picture and its fold as one coherent, memorable symbol. A light cyan underside of the fold can add one restrained secondary plane.
Composition: ONE centered logo only, straight-on, square canvas around 1024 by 1024. The background of the icon itself is a smoothly rounded square in the blue family of the reference, a restrained cyan-blue to cobalt transition if useful, not a glossy rendering. Leave a modest even transparent margin around the rounded square. White folded-picture symbol is optically centered with generous internal breathing room.
Style: polished minimalist software brand mark, crisp vector-like edges, strong silhouette, precise geometric construction, carefully balanced negative space, broad enough to remain legible at 32 pixels. Flat graphic design, just two or three broad color areas; a subtle blue transition only within the blue base is acceptable.
Background: actual transparent alpha outside the rounded-square icon; do not draw a checkerboard, white card, shadow, studio background or border around the canvas. The blue base makes the same icon usable against both light and dark interfaces.
Text: NONE. This is the standalone app symbol, with no letters, wordmark, label or slogan.
Avoid: four separate arrows, the old crowded four-corner composition, tiny decorative pixels, generic standalone mountain icon without the folding idea, a stack of unrelated symbols, camera lens, download arrow, zip fastener, paper airplane, multiple logo options, presentation board, device mockup, 3D extrusion, realistic paper texture, bevels, glow, drop shadows, watermark.
Deliver one finished original logo image.
```

## 透明边缘修整提示词

以首轮输出为编辑目标，调用同一内置生图模型。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Input image 1 is the exact PixoFold logo to clean up.
Change ONLY the outer edge and transparent surrounding area. Preserve the logo design exactly: same square composition, same blue rounded-square base, same large white folded image-file symbol, same light-cyan triangular folded corner, same blue circular sun and mountain-shaped cutout, same proportions and colors. Do not redesign or add text.
The current image has obvious stray cyan/blue speckles, irregular debris and a colored fringe OUTSIDE the rounded-square perimeter, especially around the bottom, left and right sides. REMOVE all of that. The rounded-square silhouette must be one smooth, geometrically clean, continuous edge with normal narrow antialiasing. Everything outside that clean boundary must have genuine transparent alpha. No colored pixels floating in the margin, no halo, no shadow, no checkerboard illustration, no black or white matte.
Keep a small even fully transparent margin on all sides. The symbol and blue surface inside the boundary must remain fully opaque and unchanged. Output one clean PNG logo with transparent surroundings.
```

## 生图阶段采用的不透明蓝底版本

透明背景稿经模型修整后仍有边缘杂色，因此通过同一生图模型将蓝色背景扩展至完整方形画布，保存为 [PixoFold-logo.png](PixoFold-logo.png)，尺寸 1254×1254，RGB PNG，无透明通道。该文件作为后续 SVG 复刻的参考；中间的透明背景稿未选为项目资产。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Input image 1 is the exact PixoFold logo design to preserve.
Make a clean OPAQUE square app-icon master, changing ONLY the blue background boundary and canvas coverage.
Extend the existing blue background to fill the ENTIRE square canvas, edge to edge and into all four corners. The blue must smoothly continue the existing cyan-blue at upper left to cobalt-blue at lower right. There is no rounded-square boundary, no outer margin, no separate backdrop and no transparent area anywhere. Every pixel of the final image must be fully opaque. This removes the stray pixels around the previous transparent perimeter.
Preserve the central white picture-file silhouette, cyan folded corner, circular blue sun, blue mountain cutout, their exact scale, position and proportions. Preserve the generous blue space around the white symbol. Keep the surface smooth and flat, with clean crisp geometric edges, no texture or visual noise.
Return ONE finished square blue app icon, full bleed, with the centered folded-picture symbol. NO alpha transparency, NO rounded clipping of the blue base, NO white or black margins, NO checkerboard, NO shadow, NO text, NO mockup, NO watermark.
```
